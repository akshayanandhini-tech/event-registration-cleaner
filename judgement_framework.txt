JUDGMENT FRAMEWORK
==================

Purpose:
To define how the Event Registration Cleaner decides whether a
registration is valid, needs fixing, or is a duplicate.

1. VALID
--------
A record is marked VALID when:

- Name is present.
- Phone number is valid.
- Email is valid.
- Year is between 1 and 4.
- Branch is one of the allowed branches.
- The record is not a duplicate.

Action:
The record is added to clean_list.csv.


2. NEEDS_FIX
------------
A record is marked NEEDS_FIX when:

- A required field is missing.
- Phone number is invalid.
- Email is invalid.
- Year is invalid.
- Branch is unknown or invalid.
- Fact-checking finds a mismatch between the extracted data
  and the original registration entry.

Action:
The record is added to problems.csv with the reason.


3. DUPLICATE
------------
A record is marked DUPLICATE when:

- Its cleaned phone number matches a previous valid/processed
  registration, OR
- Its cleaned email matches a previous registration.

First occurrence is kept.
Later occurrence is marked as duplicate.

Action:
The duplicate record is added to problems.csv with the reason.


4. HUMAN CHECKPOINT
-------------------
The event team reviews all records in problems.csv.

They contact the participant when information is missing,
unclear, invalid, or duplicated.

The human team makes the final decision when the automated
system cannot determine the correct information.


5. LLM ROLE
-----------
The LLM is used only for extracting information from messy
registration text.

The LLM must not guess, correct, or invent information.

Python performs fact-checking, cleaning, validation,
duplicate detection, and final classification.
